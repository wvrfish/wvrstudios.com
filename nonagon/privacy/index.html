Nonagon privacy policy

Last updated 27 September 2026

Nonagon is made by WVR Studios. In short: we don't collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. This page explains what the app does store, and where.

Your progress

Your solved puzzles, times, moves and difficulty ratings are saved on your device. If you use iCloud, Nonagon also keeps a copy in your private iCloud storage so your progress follows you to your other devices. That copy belongs to your Apple Account; WVR Studios cannot see or access it. You can turn syncing off at any time in Nonagon's Settings, or delete the data from iCloud in your device's iCloud settings.

Puzzles

Each day's puzzles are generated on your device. The app doesn't download puzzles or send your answers anywhere.

Purchases

Nonagon Plus is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and we never see your payment details. We only learn, on your device, whether a subscription is active so the app can unlock it.

Usage information

The app keeps a simple log of events, such as a puzzle being opened or solved, to help with development. That log stays on your device and isn't sent to us or to anyone else.

Children

Nonagon is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If Nonagon ever starts collecting information, this policy will be updated before that happens, and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@wvrstudios.com
