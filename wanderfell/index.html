Every step takes you somewhere

Every step you take moves you along a real route, drawn right there in your widget: up Everest, along Hadrian's Wall, across Spain on the Camino, or all the way to the Moon. The sky turns from dawn to dusk as your day goes on, and each journey costs the steps it really would. Catbells is an afternoon. The West Highland Way takes a few weeks. The Moon is a lifetime.

What's in it

  • 26 packs of real peaks and trails: the Seven Summits, the Munros, the Camino, the Tour du Mont Blanc, the Pacific Crest Trail, and dives and descents to the bottom of the sea and deep into the Earth.
  • Two lifetime walks: to the Moon, and around the world on Phileas Fogg's route.
  • Small, medium and large widgets, plus the Lock Screen.
  • Four looks: Alpine, Classic, Nightline and Synth.
  • Real ridgelines, local sunrise and sunset, seasons and winter snow.
  • In English, German, French, Spanish and Italian.
  • No account, no ads, no subscription. Your steps never leave your phone.

How it works

  • Steps come from Health, including your Apple Watch, and from your iPhone's motion sensors. On climbs, stairs count too.
  • Each climb costs the steps to walk its route plus about 10 for every metre up, after Naismith's rule. Trails cost their distance.
  • Steps beyond the finish carry on to the next journey.
  • First Steps, Seven Summits and the lifetime walks are free. Every other pack is a one-off purchase, or unlock them all, including packs still to come.
