Wanderfell press kit

Your everyday walk, turned into an epic journey.

Wanderfell, from WVR Studios, turns the steps you take every day into real journeys on your iPhone's home screen. Each step moves you along a real route, drawn in your widget: up Everest by its real ridgeline, along Hadrian's Wall, across Spain on the Camino, to the bottom of the Mariana Trench or all the way to the Moon. Every journey costs the steps it really would, worked out with the rule hillwalkers have planned by since 1892, so Catbells is an afternoon and the West Highland Way a few weeks. There are 26 packs of peaks and trails, two lifetime walks, four looks and widgets for the home and Lock Screens, in English, German, French, Spanish and Italian, with no account, no ads and no subscription. Steps come from Health and the iPhone's motion sensors and never leave the phone.
