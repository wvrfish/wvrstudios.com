Wanderfell privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

Wanderfell is made by WVR Studios. In short: we don't collect any personal data. Your steps stay on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. This page explains what the app uses, and where it stays.

Steps and flights climbed

Wanderfell reads your steps and flights climbed from Health (including your Apple Watch) and from your iPhone's motion sensors, if you allow it. It uses them on your device to move you along your journey. It never writes to or changes your Health data, and it never sends your steps or flights anywhere.

Location

If you turn on "Local sunrise & sunset" and allow it, Wanderfell reads your approximate location once, to match the sky in its pictures to your local sunrise and sunset. It's kept on your device, where the widgets can use it, and never sent anywhere. Turning the setting off forgets it. Without it, the app works out sun times from your time zone.

Your progress

Your logbook, journeys and settings are saved on your device, where the app and its widgets share them.

Purchases

Packs and looks are sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and we never see your payment details. The app only checks, on your device, which purchases you own so it can unlock them.

No network requests

Wanderfell makes no network requests of its own. There are no analytics, no accounts, no ads and no third-party SDKs.

Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may pass us anonymous crash reports. That's Apple's opt-in sharing; Wanderfell itself sends nothing.

Children

Wanderfell is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If any of this changes, this policy will be updated before the app that changes it is released, and the date at the top will change.
