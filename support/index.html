nonagon.

My progress isn't on my other device

Progress syncs through iCloud. Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account, that iCloud is on for Nonagon (Settings → your name → iCloud), and that "Sync with iCloud" is on in Nonagon's own Settings. You can tap "Sync Now" there to sync straight away.

How do I restore Nonagon Plus?

Open the Nonagon Plus screen and tap "Restore Subscription". Make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account that made the purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open Settings → your name → Subscriptions, choose Nonagon Plus and tap Cancel. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

Which games are free?

Three of the nine games are free each day, and they change daily. Nonagon Plus unlocks all nine, plus the archive of past puzzles.

Wanderfell

My steps aren't counting

Wanderfell reads steps and flights climbed from Health. Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Wanderfell and check that Steps and Flights Climbed are turned on. It can also read your iPhone's motion sensors: check Settings → Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness.

If the widget isn't moving, open Wanderfell once. iPhone only lets an app read Health while it's unlocked, so the widget shows the count the app last saw, and catches up as soon as you open the app or unlock your phone.

How are journeys costed?

Each journey costs the steps it would really take. Climbs use Naismith's rule, which hillwalkers have planned days with since 1892: the steps to walk the route, plus about 10 steps for every metre you go up. Trails and pilgrimages cost their distance. On climbs, stairs count too: each flight is about 3 m up, worth 30 steps. Tap "How costs work" in the app to see the sums for a few journeys.

How do I restore my packs and looks?

Open Settings in Wanderfell and tap "Restore purchases". Make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Can my family use my packs?

Yes. Packs, looks and All Packs work with Family Sharing, so anyone in your Family Sharing group can use them on their own devices once they've turned on purchase sharing.
