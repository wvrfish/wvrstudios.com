Support

We read every message.

Email support@wvrstudios.com with a question, a bug or an idea. For a bug, it helps to say which game, what happened, and your device and iOS version.

My progress isn't on my other device

Progress syncs through iCloud. Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account, that iCloud is on for Nonagon (Settings → your name → iCloud), and that "Sync with iCloud" is on in Nonagon's own Settings. You can tap "Sync Now" there to sync straight away.

How do I restore Nonagon Plus?

Open the Nonagon Plus screen and tap "Restore Subscription". Make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account that made the purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open Settings → your name → Subscriptions, choose Nonagon Plus and tap Cancel. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

Which games are free?

Three of the nine games are free each day, and they change daily. Nonagon Plus unlocks all nine, plus the archive of past puzzles.
